Enhancements

BU for Bandcamp works on top of Bandcamp's own player and cart, so everything you're used to keeps working. It just gets out of your way.

  1. A player that stays still

    The player is pinned to the bottom of the window, so it never scrolls out of view.

  2. Click to seek

    Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that point, instead of dragging a tiny handle.

  3. Keep your place

    Songs play one after another without the page scrolling back to the top.

  4. Previous and next

    Move between tracks right from the bottom bar.

  5. Buy a track in one click

    Every track gets a price field and an add-to-cart button. Set your price and add it with no page navigation.

  6. Your call

    Turn the cart buttons on or off in the extension's settings. Changes apply right away.

Private by design

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking.

BU for Bandcamp only runs on bandcamp.com, keeps two on/off settings in your browser, and adds tracks through Bandcamp's own cart, so checkout and payment stay entirely with Bandcamp.

Help

Questions.

Something else on your mind? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is this made by Bandcamp?

No. BU for Bandcamp is an independent, open source project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Bandcamp.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, and the source code is available under the GPL-3.0 license.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox (version 142 or newer). Browsers built on Firefox, such as Zen, can install the Firefox version.

Does it change what I pay?

No. You choose the price, and it has to meet the artist's minimum, just like on Bandcamp. The track goes into Bandcamp's own cart and you check out on Bandcamp as usual.

What does BU stand for?
