Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

BU for Bandcamp does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers of its own.

What the extension stores

The extension saves two on/off preferences: whether to show the add-to-cart button in the bottom player, and whether to show a price field and add-to-cart button on each track. They are kept in your browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync feature, your browser may sync these preferences across your devices through your browser account, the same way it syncs other extension settings. The developer never receives them.

Which websites it runs on and contacts

The extension only runs on pages under bandcamp.com. To show a track's minimum price, it loads that track's Bandcamp page from your browser, the same request your browser makes when you open the page yourself. It doesn't contact any other website.

Purchases

When you add a track to your cart, the extension hands it to Bandcamp's own cart on the page you're viewing. Checkout and payment happen entirely on Bandcamp and are covered by Bandcamp's privacy policy. The extension doesn't read or collect anything from checkout or payment pages.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save the two preferences above.
  • Access to bandcamp.com: to add the player and cart features to Bandcamp pages.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. GitHub may log basic request data such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
